## Overview

Nuxify needed a clearer website that could explain its services without overwhelming potential clients. The existing experience contained useful information, but visitors had difficulty understanding what the company offered and where to begin.

The goal of this sample project was to reorganize that information into a focused journey and create a visual system that felt confident, modern, and approachable.

## The challenge

The main challenge was balancing technical detail with simple language. The website needed to communicate expertise while remaining accessible to visitors without a technical background.

I focused on three problems:

- Important services were difficult to scan.
- Calls to action did not have a clear hierarchy.
- The visual system lacked consistency between sections.

## Research and direction

I reviewed competing technology websites and grouped their content patterns into navigation, services, proof, and conversion. This helped establish a simpler page structure and identify where visitors needed additional context.

The resulting direction used concise headings, short supporting paragraphs, and repeated calls to action to guide visitors through the page.

## Design solution

I created a flexible card system for services and case studies, supported by a consistent spacing and typography scale. Strong contrast and restrained color helped the primary actions stand out while keeping longer sections comfortable to read.

Reusable components also made the design easier to translate into Webflow and maintain as new content was added.

## Outcome

The finished concept presents the company more clearly and gives visitors a direct path from learning about a service to starting a conversation. The component system provides a practical foundation for future pages and campaigns.
